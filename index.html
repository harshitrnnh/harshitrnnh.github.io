Harshit Agrawal

Human-Computer Interaction Researcher • New Media Artist

i am a human computer interaction (HCI) researcher and new media artist exploring how emerging technologies of AI, including physical AI and robotics can augment and blend with human agency, embodiment, creative expression and human augmentation.

i hold a Master of Science from the MIT Media Lab (Fluid Interfaces Group, advised by Prof. Pattie Maes) and a Bachelor of Design from IIT Guwahati. Most recently, i have been working with Google Arts and Culture to build projects around AI and cultural material- serving as Google Arts and Culture’s first artist resident in India. Previously i was part of the Adobe Design Machine Intelligence and New Technologies, working on Adobe's gen AI charter across it's Creative Cloud offerings.

My research has been published across ACM UIST, IUI, TEI, UBICOMP, IDC, and IEEE Pervasive Computing. Concurrently, my artistic practice has been exhibited globally at Ars Electronica, the Museum of Tomorrow, Asia Culture Center, and NMACC. My work is also in the permanent collection of the the world's largest computer science museum and has earned me the recognition of being India's first AI Artist. i held India's first solo AI art exhibition (Exo-Stential at Emami Art) in 2021.
